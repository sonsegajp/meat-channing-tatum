# Meat Channing Tatum...

A deeply unnecessary, Clickamajigs-inspired browser toy.

**[Play the game](https://sonsegajp.github.io/meat-channing-tatum/)**

Click either pec to fill the flex-o-meter and trigger one of ten ridiculous events. Click his face or **Say Something** for a random one-liner. **Do Some Dumb Shit** triggers an event immediately.

- Generated mouth imagery animated over the original face
- Low-pitched browser text-to-speech and sound effects
- Ten events, including a torn suit, balloon deflation, tiny-head mode, disco, and multiplying Tatums
- Saved discoveries and replayable events
- Touch controls and keyboard shortcuts: **A/D** poke, **Space** talks, **C** causes chaos
- Standalone HTML with embedded artwork; download `index.html` to play offline

Speech uses a generic voice installed in your browser or operating system. Captions and mouth animation work even when speech is unavailable.

Unofficial absurdist parody with AI-edited imagery. Not affiliated with Channing Tatum or Nickelodeon. Contains strong language and extraordinarily poor judgment.
